 **Supermarket Sales Analysis & Dashboard**

** Project Overview**

This project analyzes supermarket sales data using Microsoft Excel to identify sales trends, customer behavior, product performance, tax collection, payment preferences, and branch-level performance.

The project includes data cleaning, calculated columns, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, KPI cards, slicers, and an interactive dashboard to convert raw sales data into useful business insights.



 **Dataset Description**

The dataset contains supermarket transaction information such as:

* Invoice / Transaction details
* Branch
* Customer Type
* Customer / Member information
* Gender
* Product Category
* Unit Price
* Quantity
* Date
* Payment Method
* Sales / Total Cost
* Tax Amount


The data was cleaned and transformed in Excel before creating the dashboard.

 **Business Questions**

1. Which branch generates the highest and lowest sales?

This helps identify differences in branch performance and determine where additional business attention may be required.

2. Which product categories contribute the most to sales?

This helps the supermarket understand which categories have the strongest customer demand.

3. Which month has the highest sales trend?

This helps identify high-performing periods and understand monthly sales patterns.

4. Which payment method is used for the most transactions?

This helps understand customer payment preferences and improve the payment experience.

5. How much tax is collected from supermarket sales?

This helps monitor tax contribution and understand the impact of different tax rates across product categories.

 Key Findings

* Branch performance: The dashboard highlights differences in sales performance between branches, including the lower sales performance of the Manhattan branch.
* Product performance: Sales vary across product categories, allowing the business to identify categories that contribute more strongly to revenue.
* Monthly trend: The monthly sales analysis identifies periods with higher and lower sales activity.
* Payment behavior: Transaction analysis shows which payment methods are most frequently used by customers.
* Tax collection: The dashboard tracks total tax collected and provides a category-based view of tax contribution.
* Customer analysis: Customer type/member information helps identify differences in purchasing behavior between customer groups.

** Data Cleaning & Preparation**

The following steps were performed before creating the dashboard:

* Removed or checked duplicate records.
* Standardized date formats.
* Converted text-based dates into proper date values.
* Created calculated sales/cost columns.
* Created a Tax Rate column.
* Applied 18% tax for Electronics and 5% for other categories.
* Created a Tax Amount column.
* Created sales classifications for analysis.
* Prepared data for Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts.

** Dashboard Components**

The dashboard contains:

* KPI Cards
* Total Sales
* Total Tax Collected
* Sales by Branch
* Sales by Product Category
* Monthly Sales Trend
* Payment Method Analysis
* Customer Type Analysis
* Interactive Slicers
* Pivot Charts



 **Recommendations**

Based on the dashboard analysis:

* Investigate the reasons for lower sales at underperforming branches.
* Focus marketing and promotional activities on high-performing product categories.
* Use monthly sales trends to plan promotions during weaker periods.
* Ensure sufficient inventory for high-demand categories.
* Monitor customer payment preferences when planning payment services.
* Track tax collection regularly for better financial monitoring.
